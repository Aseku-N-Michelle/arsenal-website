Our History

More than 135 years of the club, from Woolwich to the Emirates.

  • 1886

    Workers at the Royal Arsenal factory in Woolwich, London, form the club as Dial Square.

  • 1893

    Woolwich Arsenal joins the Football League.

  • 1913

    The club moves north and plays at Highbury, its home for 93 years.

  • 1930-31

    Under Herbert Chapman, Arsenal win their first league title.

  • 1970-71

    Arsenal win the league and FA Cup double for the first time.

  • 1988-89

    Michael Thomas scores in the last minute at Anfield to win the title.

  • 1997-98

    Arsène Wenger leads the club to a second double.

  • 2003-04

    The Invincibles go the whole 38-game league season unbeaten.

  • 2006

    The club moves to the Emirates Stadium.

  • 2019

    Mikel Arteta becomes manager in December.

  • 2020

    Arsenal win a record 14th FA Cup.
